📖 Sign Language Guide

Sign A

Make a closed fist facing forward with all 4 fingers folded down and thumb resting upright along the side of the index finger.

Sign B

Hold all 4 fingers straight up touching together; fold your thumb flat across the palm.

Sign C

Curve all 4 fingers and thumb to form an open 'C' shape facing sideways.

Sign D

Point your index finger straight up; touch your thumb to middle, ring, and pinky tips to make an 'O'.

Sign E

Fold all 4 fingertips down tightly against the palm; tuck the thumb flat underneath the fingers.

Sign F

Touch the index finger and thumb tips together to make a circle; extend the other 3 fingers straight up.

Sign G

Point index finger and thumb horizontally to the side parallel to each other like a mini pistol.

Sign H

Extend both index and middle fingers together horizontally to the side; tuck other fingers in.

Sign I

Make a closed fist with your pinky finger pointing straight up.

Sign J

Extend your pinky finger and trace a 'J' hook motion in the air.

Sign K

Point index and middle fingers upward in a 'V' shape, with your thumb tip resting in between them.

Sign L

Form an 'L' shape with your index finger pointing straight up and thumb extended to the side.

Sign N

Make a fist with your thumb tucked underneath your index and middle fingers.

Sign O

Curve all four fingers to meet your thumb tip, forming an 'O' ring.

Sign P

Make the 'K' sign shape oriented downward towards the floor.

Sign Q

Point your index finger and thumb downward like grasping something small.

Sign R

Cross your middle finger over your index finger (like wishing for luck).

Sign U

Hold index and middle fingers straight up touching together, with thumb tucked over ring finger.

Sign V

Hold index and middle fingers straight up separated in a peace/victory 'V' shape.

Sign W

Hold index, middle, and ring fingers straight up separated in a 'W' shape.

Sign X

Make a fist and curl your index finger into a hook/claw shape.

Sign Y

Extend your thumb and pinky outwards while curling the middle three fingers (phone gesture).

Sign SPACE [ ]

Hold open palm completely flat facing the camera with all five fingers spread.

Sign DELETE ⌫

Make a fist with your thumb pointing straight down (thumbs-down gesture).
